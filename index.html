Prompting that produces usable work

A strong teacher prompt is less about clever wording and more about complete instructional context.

01 • Context

Tell AI who and what

  • Grade, course, and time available
  • Exact benchmark and learning target
  • What students already know
02 • Evidence

Show the learning need

  • Anonymous performance patterns
  • Common errors or misconceptions
  • Student work evidence—without names
03 • Constraints

Define a useful output

  • Materials, timing, and grouping
  • Scaffolds and accessibility needs
  • Answer key and mastery criteria
Responsible AI use

The teacher stays in the driver’s seat.

Use AI to speed up drafting, comparison, and pattern-finding. Your professional judgment determines what is accurate, appropriate, equitable, and ready for students.

1De-identify data.
Never enter student names, IDs, birth dates, disability labels, or confidential records.
2Verify Florida requirements.
Confirm benchmark wording, FAST achievement ranges, and district expectations using current official sources.
3Check rigor and bias.
Review reading level, cultural assumptions, accessibility, correct answers, and alignment before use.
4Keep evidence in the loop.
Use formative checks to decide whether to reteach, regroup, extend, or revise the AI-generated draft.
